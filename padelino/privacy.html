Privacy Policy

Padelino for Android phones and Wear OS watches · Effective 18 October 2026

  • There is no account and no Padelino server. Your matches stay on your phone and watch.
  • Heart rate, calories, steps and hits are measured on your watch and stay on your devices.
  • If you turn it on, matches are saved as workouts in Health Connect. Padelino only writes there; it never reads your health data.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • No ads, no advertising ID and no tracking across apps.

Who we are

Padelino for Android is made by Tarik Fazlić, an independent developer ("we" in this policy). Contact: tarikfaz@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your devices

The matches you record or log, scores, players, team names and your settings are stored in a database inside the app on your phone. While a match is being played, the watch keeps it in the app's private storage until you finish it.

The watch and the phone talk to each other directly through Google Play services' Wear OS connection (the "Data Layer"). This is how a finished match, the live score and your player list move between them. We don't receive any of it.

If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Padelino's data in your Google backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is controlled by your Google account, not by us.

Heart rate, calories and steps

During a match the watch app measures heart rate, calories and steps with Wear OS Health Services. For this it asks for the watch's heart rate (body sensors) and physical activity permissions. The readings are saved with the match on your phone and shown in the app. Heart rate zones use the maximum heart rate you set in Settings; we don't read your date of birth or other profile data.

Active calories are worked out in the app from the total calories reported by the watch.

Health Connect

If you turn on Settings › Save Matches as Workouts, the phone app writes each match recorded on the watch to Health Connect as a workout: start and end time, heart rate, total and active calories, and steps. Other apps you allow, such as Samsung Health or Google Fit, can then show it.

  • Padelino asks for write access only. It does not read any data from Health Connect.
  • Nothing written to Health Connect is sent to us or to anyone else by Padelino.
  • You can turn the switch off at any time, and remove Padelino's access in Health Connect. Workouts already saved stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

Motion sensors and hit counting

When Hit Tracking is on, the watch reads its accelerometer and gyroscope during a match to count ball hits. This is worked out on the watch; only the number of hits is kept and saved with the match. You can turn Hit Tracking off in Settings.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which features are used and to fix crashes, the phone app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Usage statistics are anonymous events such as which screen was opened or that a match was logged. They never contain names, scores, team names, anything you type, or health data. Google also collects basic technical data, such as device model, Android version, app version and approximate country.
  • Crash reports contain technical details about the crash (device model, Android version, app version and where in the code it happened). They don't contain your matches, players or health data.
  • Padelino doesn't use the advertising ID.
  • You can turn both off with Settings › Share Usage Data.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.

Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Notifications

While a match is being played, the phone can show a notification with the live score. It is created on your phone and isn't sent anywhere.

Bug reports and email

If you use Settings › Report a Bug or write to us, we receive your email address, your message and, for bug reports, the app version and phone model that the app adds. We use them only to reply and fix the problem, and delete the email when it's no longer needed.

Exports

CSV and JSON exports are created on your phone. They go only where you choose to send or save them.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent any data. Apart from Google processing usage statistics and crash reports as described above, no data is shared with anyone.

Children

Padelino is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Delete everything in the app with Settings › Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn off Save Matches as Workouts, and manage or delete workouts in Health Connect.
  • Turn off Share Usage Data.
  • Turn off Hit Tracking.
  • Email us with any question or request.

Changes
