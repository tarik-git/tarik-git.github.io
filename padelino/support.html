Getting started

What do I need?

An Android phone (Android 8 or newer) for your matches and stats. To score on the court and record heart rate, a Wear OS watch (Wear OS 3 or newer) with Padelino installed, paired with the phone. You can also log matches on the phone alone.

How do I score a match?

On the watch, open Padelino, choose the rules (1 or 3 sets, Golden Point, Super Tiebreak) and tap Start Match. Tap Team A or Team B for each point; the watch keeps the score and vibrates for games and sets. Undo the last point with the arrow, and end early with the flag. Swipe up for heart rate, zone, calories and hits. At the end tap Done and the match goes to your phone.

What is Just Track?

For when you don't want to score point by point. The watch records time, heart rate, calories and hits; when you finish you enter the final score in sets and games (turn the crown or bezel to change a number), or save without a score.

How do I add players to a match?

Players are added on the phone. Open the match in Matches and set the team names and players. Add yourself in My Profile › Manage Players and mark yourself as Default to see your own stats.

Can I follow the match on my phone?

Yes. While a match runs on the watch, a LIVE card appears on the Matches screen and a notification with the score shows on the phone. Team A / Team B on the phone or in the notification add a point on the watch.

Common questions

My match doesn't show on my phone

  • Make sure Padelino is installed on both the phone and the watch, and the watch is connected (Galaxy Wearable or Pixel Watch app).
  • Open Padelino on the phone once. If the phone was out of range, the match arrives when the watch reconnects.

Why are my matches saved as Tennis?

Wear OS and Health Connect have no padel workout type, so Padelino uses Tennis, the closest sport in movement and energy use. The workout is titled "Padel" and has the set scores in its notes.

How do I get my matches into Samsung Health or Google Fit?

Turn on Settings › Save Matches as Workouts and allow Padelino to write to Health Connect. Then allow Samsung Health or Google Fit to read Exercise and Heart rate in Health Connect. Matches recorded on the watch after that are added as workouts.

Active or total calories?

Padelino shows active calories: what the match itself burned. The watch measures total calories (active plus what your body burns at rest), and Padelino takes off the resting part for the length of the match. The match details show both.

How are hits counted?

With Hit Tracking on, the watch's motion sensors look for the sharp jolt of the ball meeting the racket during a swing. Wear the watch on the hand you play with. If it counts too many or too few, change Settings › Sensitivity (Low, Medium, High).

How are heart rate zones worked out?

As a share of your maximum heart rate: Zone 1 below 60%, Zone 2 60–70%, Zone 3 70–80%, Zone 4 80–90%, Zone 5 above 90%. Set your own maximum in Settings › Custom Max Heart Rate.

Can I move my matches to a new phone?

With Android's backup turned on, your matches can come back when you set up a new phone with the same Google account. You can also keep a copy with Settings › Export as JSON or Export as CSV.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete All Data removes all matches and players from the phone. Workouts saved to Health Connect are deleted in Health Connect itself.

Which languages are available?

English and Bosnian. On Android 13 and newer you can pick the language for Padelino alone in Settings › Language; otherwise it follows your phone.
